One tap. Two orbits. No mercy.

Your orb races around two glowing rings. Spikes ride the rings toward you. Tap anywhere to jump between the inner and outer orbit, and switch at the very last second to score a PERFECT. Chain perfects for combo points, collect shards, and keep going: every 25 points the orbit FLIPS direction, the colors change and the speed climbs.

HOW IT PLAYS
• One-thumb controls: tap anywhere to switch orbit
• PERFECT dodges and combos reward nerve, not luck
• FLIP every 25 points: new direction, new palette, more speed
• A synthwave soundtrack generated in real time that pulses with the rings

KEEP COMING BACK
• DAILY course: a new layout every day. Play on back-to-back days to grow your streak and earn bonus shards
• ECHO mode: your last run comes back the other way round. Dodge it for +2, outlive it for +5
• Unlock 7 orb skins with the shards you collect, from EMBER to the color-shifting PRISM

MADE FOR PHONES
• Plays offline, no account needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected
• Pause any time; the game pauses itself if a call comes in
• Haptics and sound can be turned off in the menu

How far can you get?
